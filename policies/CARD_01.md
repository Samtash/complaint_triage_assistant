# CARD_01: Lost or Stolen Card

*Sample policy written for this project. Not a real bank policy.*

## What the customer should do
* Call the 24 hour helpline or block the card in the mobile app right away.
* Report any payments they did not make.

## What the bank does
* Blocks the card as soon as the customer reports it.
* Sends a new card within 7 working days.
* Checks all payments made after the time the customer says the card was lost.

## Who pays for unauthorized payments
* Payments made after the card was reported lost: the bank covers them.
* Payments made before the report: the bank reviews each case under CARD_02.

## Fees
* Replacement card: BDT 500. This fee is waived if the card was stolen and the customer has a police report or general diary (GD) copy.
