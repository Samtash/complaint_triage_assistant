# CARD_02: Disputed Card Payment

*Sample policy written for this project. Not a real bank policy.*

## When this applies
The customer sees a card payment they did not make, a double charge or a wrong amount.

## What the customer should do
* Report the payment within 30 days of the statement date.
* Share the date, amount and merchant name of the payment.

## What the bank does
* Logs the dispute and gives the customer a reference number on the same day.
* For suspected fraud, blocks the card right away and follows CARD_01.
* Contacts the merchant's bank to check the payment.
* May give a temporary credit for the disputed amount within 10 working days while the case is checked.

## Timelines
* Most disputes are settled within 45 days.
* The customer is updated at least every 15 days.
