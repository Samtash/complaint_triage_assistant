# ACC_01: Opening an Account and Documents (KYC)

*Sample policy written for this project. Not a real bank policy.*

## Documents needed
* National ID card (NID) or passport
* Two recent passport size photos
* Proof of address, such as a utility bill from the last 3 months
* Nominee's NID and photo

## Timelines
* Savings account: opened within 2 working days once all documents are correct.
* If a document is missing or unclear, the bank tells the customer within 1 working day.

## Updating details
* Customers must update their details if their address, phone number or ID changes.
* The bank may ask customers to update their documents every 5 years.
