# LOAN_01: Loan Repayment and Late Fees

*Sample policy written for this project. Not a real bank policy.*

## Repayment
* Installments are due on the date shown in the loan agreement.
* Customers can pay at a branch, by auto debit from their account or through the mobile app.

## Late payment
* A late fee of 2% of the missed installment is charged after a 5 day grace period.
* The bank sends an SMS reminder 3 days before each due date and again on the day after a missed payment.

## Customers in difficulty
* Customers who cannot pay because of illness, job loss or a similar problem can ask for a repayment plan review.
* The bank replies to these requests within 7 working days.

## Early repayment
* Customers can repay early. A fee of 1% of the remaining amount may apply in the first year of the loan.
