# DIG_02: Failed Transfer but Money Deducted

*Sample policy written for this project. Not a real bank policy.*

## When this applies
The customer made a transfer through the app or internet banking. The money left their account but did not reach the receiver.

## What the bank does
* Most failed transfers are reversed automatically within 3 working days.
* If the money is not back after 3 working days, the customer should report it with the date, amount and transaction ID.
* The bank logs the case and gives a reference number on the same day.
* The bank checks with the receiving bank and returns the money within 10 working days of the report.

## Updates
* The customer gets an SMS when the case is opened and when the money is returned.
