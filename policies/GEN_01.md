# GEN_01: How the Bank Handles Complaints

*Sample policy written for this project. Not a real bank policy.*

## Ways to complain
* Branch, helpline, email or the mobile app.

## Timelines
* Every complaint gets a reference number within 1 working day.
* Simple complaints are solved within 7 working days.
* Complex complaints are solved within 30 days, with an update at least every 15 days.

## Escalation
* If the customer is not happy with the answer, they can ask for the complaint to go to the Complaint Management Cell.
* Fraud and lost card complaints are always handled first.

## Tone
* Staff must reply politely and in plain language, explain what will happen next and give the reference number.
