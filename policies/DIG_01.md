# DIG_01: Mobile App Login and OTP Problems

*Sample policy written for this project. Not a real bank policy.*

## Common problems
* Forgotten password
* One time password (OTP) not received
* Account locked after too many wrong attempts

## What the customer can do
* Reset the password with the "Forgot password" option in the app.
* Check that their registered phone number is correct and has signal.
* Wait 30 minutes. Accounts locked after wrong attempts unlock on their own.

## What the bank does
* If the OTP still does not arrive, the helpline checks the registered number and resends it.
* If the phone number has changed, the customer must update it at a branch with their NID.
* The bank never asks for a password or OTP by phone, SMS or email.
