# ACC_02: Frozen or Dormant Accounts

*Sample policy written for this project. Not a real bank policy.*

## Dormant accounts
* An account becomes dormant if there is no customer transaction for 12 months.
* To reactivate it, the customer visits any branch with their NID and signs a reactivation form.
* Reactivation is done within 1 working day. There is no fee.

## Frozen accounts
* An account may be frozen because of a court or regulator order, missing KYC documents or suspected fraud.
* The bank tells the customer the general reason, unless the law does not allow it.
* If the reason is missing documents, the account is unfrozen within 2 working days after the documents are submitted.
* Frozen accounts because of legal orders can only be released by the same authority.
